1. As an administrator, open **Contacts → Configuration → Localization → Cities** and search
   for a CAP: the Italian localities of that postal code are listed with their province.

   ![Cities list filtered on the CAP 39100: nine localities of the province Bolzano (IT)](static/description/en/01_lista_localitati_cap.png)

2. Open an Italian contact and type the CAP in the address: the city and the province are
   filled in.

   ![Contact in Italy: the CAP 20121 fills in the city Milano (20121) and the state Milano (IT)](static/description/en/02_partener_cap_unic.png)

3. When the CAP is shared by several localities, only the province is filled in; choose the
   city from the list (type the CAP or the name in the city field).

   ![Contact in Italy: the CAP 39100 fills in the state Bolzano (IT); the city field lists the localities of 39100](static/description/en/03_partener_cap_comun.png)

4. To complete existing or imported contacts, select them in the list and run
   *Actions → Italy: set city from ZIP*.

   ![Contacts list with Italian contacts selected and the Actions menu open on Italy: set city from ZIP](static/description/en/04_actiune_contacte_existente.png)

   ![Contact Cliente Roma after the action: ZIP 00187, city Roma (00187) and state Roma (IT)](static/description/en/05_contact_dupa_actiune.png)
